# Welcome to the `temporal` Documentation!

This is your guide to mastering the `temporal` framework. Whether you're a beginner looking to make your first forecast or an expert looking to build a custom, state-of-the-art model, you'll find everything you need here.

## 🗺️ Where to Go Next

* **New to `temporal`?** Start with the **[Getting Started Tutorial](./getting-started.md)**. It will walk you through the process of building and training your first model in just a few minutes.
* **Want to understand how `temporal` works?** Check out the **[Core Concepts](./core-concepts.md)** guide. You'll learn about the configuration-driven design, the registry, and the builder pattern that make `temporal` so flexible.
* **Ready to unlock the full power of the Transformer?** The **[Transformer Capabilities](./transformer-capabilities.md)** guide provides a deep dive into the advanced features of the Transformer module, including the different attention mechanisms, architectural flexibility, and probabilistic forecasting capabilities.
* **Want to build your own custom components?** The **[Extending `temporal`](./extending-temporal.md)** guide shows you how to add your own custom attention mechanisms, output heads, and more.
